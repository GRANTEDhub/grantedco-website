IntellEngine is GRANTED's SME-trained grant intelligence platform. On Platform it is fully automated and self-serve: matching, scored alerts, GrantBot, and the AI Proposal Developer, with no human review. Partner and Enterprise add GRANTED's senior grant strategists and expert verification. Every client's full funding picture in a single workspace — what's matched, in pursuit, decided, and coming due, with the tools to act on it built in.

How It Works
From your profile to grants worth pursuing

Most platforms match on keywords, surfacing hundreds of loosely aligned grants. IntellEngine matches and scores against a profile built around your organization's real funding priorities. Every match comes with an AI-generated concept proposal, so you understand the grant and how to pursue it. On Platform, matching and proposals are fully automated with no human review; Partner and Enterprise add verification by a GRANTED expert.
