How It Works
From your profile to grants worth pursuing

Most platforms match on keywords, surfacing hundreds of loosely aligned grants. IntellEngine matches and scores against a profile built around your organization's real funding priorities. Every grant is verified by a GRANTED expert and comes with a concept proposal — so you understand the grant and how to pursue it.
